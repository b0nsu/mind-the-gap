# Vendor notes

## Datadog
- Plan: annual, prepaid. Current term ends 2027-02-28. Cancelling before the term ends does not refund the remaining months.
- Log retention: 15 days on the plan. Logs are not exported anywhere else.
- Dashboards: 23, built by the SRE team over two years. Export: JSON per dashboard via the API.
- Cancellation: account owner opens a ticket with support; takes effect at the end of the current month.
