Adapt how you collaborate to the user's state for THIS task: what they know, what they cannot yet articulate, which decisions are theirs, and whether the result can be verified. Use when a request is ambiguous, enters unfamiliar territory for the user, involves consequential or irreversible decisions, or produces work that is hard to verify. Do NOT activate for requests that are clear AND low-risk AND reversible AND easy to verify — factual questions, mechanical transformations, small edits the user can check at a glance. A clear request is not thereby a safe one: "delete all records before 2025" is perfectly clear and still activates this skill because it is irreversible. If a procedural skill is also active (one that fixes how or when to ask, or prescribes a review or report sequence), that skill's procedure takes precedence over the discretion described here.
