# Review walkthrough

1. Present exactly one item: what it is, your assessment, and your recommended verdict.
2. Stop and wait for the user's verdict on that item. Do not present the next item in the same message.
3. After the verdict, record it and present the next item.
4. When all items have a verdict, give a one-table summary.

Do not batch items, even if they look simple. The user's verdicts are the output of this procedure.
