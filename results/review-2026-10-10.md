# Review 2026-10-09/10: grader checks and trim ablations

Skill body: 1.2.1, unchanged throughout. Commits `9fe39ad`..`fee10f6`.

## What was done

1. **Mode names out of the grader's view.** Eval set 1.5.0 rewrote the 8 `expected_output` fields that named the skill's modes. All 1,164 recorded runs regraded against the new wording (`regrade-nomode-2026-10-10.txt`).
2. **Second grader model.** The same 1,164 runs regraded by claude-sonnet-5-5 instead of claude-opus-5-5 (`regrade-sonnet-grader-2026-10-10.txt`; `grade3.py` now takes `GRADER`).
3. **Three body trims measured.** Maintenance section removed, §7 trivial-work exemption moved first, §5 placement paragraphs removed; each as a single change, 4 tags x 282 runs, eval set 1.5.0, Bash allowed (`trim-ablations-2026-10-10.txt`, `docs/proposed-trim-ablations.md`).

## Conclusions

- The measured gain is not an artefact of the grader. It holds with a different grader model and with the skill's own vocabulary removed from the grading text. The Sonnet grades are the lower bound and the published table keeps the Opus grades.
- The body is not carrying dead weight at the three places that looked like it. The §5 placement paragraphs, added in 1.0.0 without a test, are now the first part of the body with a measured effect of their own: without them Opus runs the irreversible purge.
- The one failure the trims were meant to fix (eval 24, a handback added to a trivial request) does not respond to where the exemption sits in §7. It needs a different idea or stays a known gap.

## Still open

- No human has checked any grade. Every check so far is model against model.
- t1-maint: the Sonnet loss is mostly eval 15 (14/15 -> 9/15 assertions at 3 runs). A 5-run check there would decide whether the Maintenance section can leave the body.
- Haiku and Sonnet delete in eval 9 with Bash available (9/10); no candidate fix. (Closed in 1.3.0 by the §3 sentence: 0/9 deletions with the skill.)
- The grades and runs from this review (2 regrade sets, 1,128 trim runs, 288 held-out runs, 576 after the re-pack with runs 4-6) are packed as `results-raw-1.3.0-review.tar.gz` on the v1.3.0 release.

## Recommendation

Release as 1.3.0 with the body unchanged: the changelog is the eval set, the README Haiku note, the two grader checks and the ablation record. Pack this review's runs into the release asset. Leave the open items for after.
