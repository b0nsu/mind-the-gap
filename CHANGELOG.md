## 1.2.0 (2026-10-08)
- §2 CO-CREATE rewritten: an observable cue (a request to produce something with a direction and no criteria), options that differ in structure or framing, and two rules — missing facts do not postpone the options (use placeholders), and a list of intake questions, even with defaults, is not a substitute for options (§4 applies after the mode is chosen). Driven by eval 11 failing on all three models in both configurations: models wrote one finished document or asked 4-6 intake questions.
- §2 DISCOVER: sort unknowns into what you can research now and what only the user or a third party holds; do not hand the user a research task you could run. Driven by eval 10.
- evals.json 1.2.0: eval 10 assertion 1 now forbids only an unconditional verdict (the grader had passed and failed equally conditional first sentences); eval 11 assertion 2 accepts any request to react to the options.
- Measured (with skill, 3 runs per eval): Haiku 85%, Sonnet 94%, Opus 92% (1.1.0 regraded: 79%, 90%, 92%); without 66%, 69%, 73%. Eval 11 failures 6/9 -> 0/9 on both target assertions; eval 10 research/user split 5/9 -> 0/9.
- Evals 9 and 12 looked worse at 3 runs; rerun at 5 runs per model on 1.1.0 and 1.2.0 they were unchanged (eval 9: 7/15 vs 6/15 runs failed; eval 12: 7/15 vs 7/15). Recorded as noise.
- Words with skill rose on eval 11 (Sonnet +70%, Opus +92% over without) because the response now carries two or three options. Totals with skill vs without: Haiku -7%, Sonnet +6%, Opus +1%.
- The explicit-invocation proposal (docs/proposed-1.3-explicit-invocation.md) is now targeted at 1.3.0.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-08)
- §4: when more than one question remains, attach a default to each so the user can settle all of them in one reply. Driven by eval 3 failing "every decision comes with a default" 9/9 with and without the skill across three models; with this sentence 5/9.
- Two other candidate sentences (§3: reversible-but-product-behavior choices proceed with a default marked as the user's call; §5: when proceeding without an answer, say how the work survives a change of mind) were measured and dropped. The §5 sentence did not move eval 15 (7/9 → 6/9 failures). The §3 sentence was first suspected of an eval-6 regression, but the same failures appeared without it, so the drop is for lack of effect, not for harm.
- evals.json 1.1.0: assertions rewritten for evals 3, 5, 13, 15 where they forced a form rather than the behaviour (eval 3 allows principle-level advice first; eval 5 accepts a question or a default marked as the user's call; eval 13 checks that the decision is not claimed rather than requiring an ownership sentence; eval 15 counts consequences as "at least one"). 1.0.1 responses regraded under the new assertions: with skill 81% / 87% / 89% (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus), without 66% / 69% / 71%.
- Measured (3 runs per eval, so differences of one or two runs are noise): with skill 78% / 89% / 91%. Total words with skill are now about equal to without (-1% / -2% / -5%), down from -12% / -5% / -10% at 1.0.1; the per-eval increases are driven by single long runs and were not investigated further.
